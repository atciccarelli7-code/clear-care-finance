# Monetization and Conversion

## Mandate

Ensure the platform captures sustainable economic value at moments where it can genuinely help the user take the next step. Monetization must follow user value, remain clearly disclosed, and never distort calculations or editorial conclusions.

## Revenue lenses

Inspect relevant work for:

- affiliate or referral partnerships
- qualified lead generation
- email capture tied to a valuable deliverable
- premium decision systems or reports
- institutional licensing, sponsorship, or education programs
- advertising eligibility and placement
- services or courses that fit the audience and founder capability

## Workflow

1. Map the user's intent from entry through decision and next action.
2. Identify high-intent moments, especially after users enter personal assumptions, receive estimated savings, compare benefits, or confront a coverage gap.
3. Locate dead ends, premature external handoffs, and valuable actions currently performed outside the platform.
4. Define the user benefit that must exist before any conversion request.
5. Evaluate possible revenue models for relevance, trust, economics, implementation burden, approval requirements, and concentration risk.
6. Research current program terms from primary or direct program sources before recommending a partner.
7. Design balanced decision logic that includes reasons not to use the monetized option.
8. Specify disclosure, placement, alternative resources, and editorial separation.
9. Define funnel events from eligibility or intent through impression, click, lead, approval, purchase, and downstream value where measurable.
10. Estimate economics with explicit traffic, conversion, commission, retention, and uncertainty assumptions.
11. Design experiments that can distinguish value creation from dark-pattern conversion.
12. Check whether the same user need is better served through a premium product, saved report, or internal resource rather than an affiliate link.

## Required output

Return:

- `Status`: `PASS`, `WARN`, `BLOCK`, or `NOT IMPLICATED`
- `High-intent moment`
- `Current dead end or leakage`
- `User value before conversion`
- `Recommended revenue mechanism`
- `Reasons the user should not convert`
- `Disclosure and editorial-separation requirements`
- `Economics and assumptions`
- `Funnel instrumentation`
- `Experiment and stopping rule`

## Conversion principles

- Result first, interpretation second, appropriate next action third, monetized option fourth.
- Commercial options must be relevant to the user's demonstrated need.
- Affiliate compensation must not influence formulas, rankings, or eligibility logic.
- Users must retain access to official and noncommercial alternatives.
- Email capture must deliver a durable benefit such as saved results, a personalized checklist, decision brief, or update the user requested.
- Advertising is supplemental inventory, not the platform's strategic center.

## Guardrails

- Do not add generic provider links to every related page.
- Do not recommend refinancing federal loans without prominently addressing lost federal protections.
- Do not imply endorsement or guaranteed savings.
- Do not obscure sponsorship, ranking methodology, or eligibility limits.
- Do not optimize click-through rate at the expense of decision quality.
- Do not rely on one affiliate or ad network as the only viable business model.

## Completion test

The role passes only when the full high-intent journey was inspected, economic leakage was considered, the recommended model creates clear user value, conflicts are disclosed and controlled, alternatives remain available, and funnel performance can be measured without compromising trust.
