## Reviewed but intentionally ad-free

Twenty-seven articles remain ad-free because they address Medicare, Medicaid, medication safety, hospital discharge, prior authorization, financial assistance, or another consequential patient decision. Five additional articles remain ad-free because they are reference-led, freshness-heavy, duplicative, or directory-like.

## Permanently ad-free surfaces

- calculators and guided workflows;
- personalized results, saved plans, receipts, and private application routes;
- forms and newsletter capture;
- trust, methodology, legal, privacy, and disclosure pages;
- hubs, directories, topic guides, and printables;
- unknown and future routes until affirmatively reviewed.

## Policy basis

Google reviews the entire connected site during approval and supports page and section exclusions for Auto ads. Community Acquired Finance therefore uses affirmative route-level publisher eligibility rather than broad `/articles/` or `/topics/` prefixes. Eligible articles must pass publication-quality checks and remain outside interactive or sensitive contexts.

This repository audit does not submit the site for AdSense review, change `ads.txt`, change the sitemap, request indexing, or modify account-level ad settings.
