## Scoring method

Total is out of 50. A high total does not override a duplicate-intent, privacy, or evidence gate.

## Selection rationale

The selected portfolio protects three different reader jobs rather than publishing three versions of “hospitals need money”:

1. the institutional contradiction behind hospital readiness;
2. the decision boundary between clinical recommendation and coverage approval; and
3. the accounting distinction between institution, payer, service, and case economics.

Candidate 2 will replace the published article object at `/articles/prior-authorization-explained` while preserving the URL, early search equity, and existing links. Candidates 1 and 3 have distinct search intent and no current CAF canonical. Candidates 4–10 remain protected source material, not a content quota.
