# CAF Research Intelligence System

Established August 3, 2026.

## Purpose

Community Acquired Finance uses a research-first operating model. Material website changes should not begin until the relevant evidence, user need, decision logic, technical constraints, and maintenance obligations are documented.

## Platform responsibilities

- **Notion:** research synthesis, product reasoning, decision records, and knowledge maps.
- **Google Drive:** original source files, PDFs, datasets, exports, snapshots, and interview evidence.
- **GitHub:** build-ready schemas, source registries, decision rules, annual values, calculator specifications, and technical documentation.
- **Linear:** actionable research gaps, validation tasks, and build-readiness gates.

## Required pre-build evidence

Every material implementation should identify:

1. User and decision served.
2. Authoritative sources and effective dates.
3. Rules, thresholds, exceptions, and uncertainty.
4. Frontline or user evidence where relevant.
5. Existing routes, tools, data, and architecture affected.
6. Required inputs, outputs, formulas, and decision logic.
7. Accessibility, privacy, legal, and trust implications.
8. Review cadence and change-detection sources.
9. Build-readiness status: ready, partially ready, or research blocked.

## Research domains

- Healthcare policy, insurance, benefits, tax, retirement, and public programs
- Hospital operations and patient financial workflows
- Healthcare occupations and career economics
- UX, accessibility, information architecture, and trust
- Software architecture, data modeling, calculators, search, analytics, and testing
- Competitive landscape, business models, and market gaps
- Frontline interviews and founder clinical evidence

## Quality standard

A report alone is not sufficient. Research must be source-backed, dated, structured for reuse, explicit about uncertainty, and capable of informing content, code, calculations, or product decisions.
