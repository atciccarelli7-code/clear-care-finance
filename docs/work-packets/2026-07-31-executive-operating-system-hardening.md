## Failure being corrected

The July 29 AdSense remediation reduced ad eligibility from ten routes to five. The later multi-role system inherited that implementation as a trusted safety baseline. It verified that the restriction worked but did not challenge whether five of roughly 160 indexable routes—about 3.1%—was commercially coherent. Repeated editorial work was also lost because absence from one hard-coded advertising registry was treated as if the work had never occurred.

The prior system had broad role coverage but lacked four enforceable controls:

1. inherited-decision challenge;
2. quantified numerator-and-denominator impact;
3. anomaly escalation;
4. separate technical and business validation.
