## Quantified impact and boundaries

- Covers for flagships stay at 6/6; illustrated stories in the library go from six to eight. Original illustrations have descriptive alt and AI-assistance attribution. The recent stories add 787,489 bytes in stored derivatives; thumbnails total 47,248 bytes. Each image uses one responsive candidate.
- Homepage keeps all nine existing featured/flagship destinations. It keeps three direct links to figures and moves the figure section up before the flagship shelf. Thirteen existing figures remain discoverable through the archive fragment.
- The main shared article view uses the editorial heading and recommendations. Article data, canonical inventory, structured data types, claims, citations, SEO titles, and user workflows retain their existing ownership. Build counts 192 public canonicals, 192 sitemap URLs and 39 permanent redirects. No archive reclassification.
- The redesign affects more than one fifth of the principal reading surface by intent. Preserve the digital-publication grid and text alternatives, and test at 320px as well as desktop. Keep the chart essay’s first passage before the illustration. Reversible as one normal PR revert, without a migration or configuration changes.
- Article and explainer navigation events reuse first-party consent and publish only editorial identifiers. Compare navigation and settled reading cohorts over 28 days. Exposure, time on story and search ranking must be evaluated separately. No outcome is inferred from a release.
